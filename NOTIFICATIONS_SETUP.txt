PHONE NOTIFICATION SETUP

1. Install the free "ntfy" app on the phone.
2. Open the Streamlit app > Profile > Notifications.
3. Tap "Generate a private notification topic".
4. Copy the generated topic and subscribe to that exact topic in the ntfy phone app.
5. Turn on "Enable phone push notifications" in the Streamlit app and save.
6. Tap "Send test phone notification". A notification should appear on the phone.

The Streamlit app can send notifications while it is running.
For reminders to arrive automatically when nobody has the app open, run notifier.py hourly on an always-on scheduler/host. It uses the same carer_data.json and will not send the same shift reminder twice.

Default shift-day reminder:
"You have a shift today 🌙 Your shift starts at 8:00 PM at Earlswood. Don’t forget your water and apple juice so you stay hydrated. 💧🍎"

You can edit both the reminder time and message in Profile > Notifications.
